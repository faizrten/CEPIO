# Glossary

**Product terminology authority:** [CEPIO Product North Star & Strategic Doctrine](../strategy/industry-iq-product-north-star.md). **Industrial Competence Network** means versioned, permissioned, independently qualified reusable industrial competence and assurance—not pooled enterprise customer data. **Industry IQ Companion** means the proposed persona-adaptive professional experience through approved host surfaces; it is not a new CEPIO agent runtime. **Intelligence sovereignty** means customer-specific operational data and derivative knowledge remain within authorized customer control.

**Action** — Governed operation that changes CEPIO state or causes an external side effect. Action authority is separate from reasoning/recommendation authority.

**Actor** — Authenticated human, application, service/workload or AI identity evaluated by CEPIO policy and authority contracts.

**Application Contract** — Protocol-neutral CEPIO contract defining stable caller-visible industrial context, Skill, Decision and Action-intent semantics. REST, SDK and MCP are projections over it.

**Assurance** — Evidence that a scoped CEPIO semantic/runtime/Skill/agent/operational claim behaves as required. Assurance is distinct from generalization.

**Binding** — Connection between CEPIO's provider-neutral industrial contract and an external platform/source representation.

**Binding Contract** — Source/provider-neutral contract for reading evidence/context and, where authorized, executing/verifying/reconciling governed external effects without making provider schemas canonical CEPIO semantics.

**Bitemporal** — Modeling with both valid time and transaction time.

**Capability** — Evidence-backed statement that CEPIO can support a defined industrial function/question/procedure under declared conditions.

**Capability Registry** — Governed registry of what CEPIO can legitimately claim it knows how to do, where it has been proven, and what evidence/maturity supports that claim.

**Canonical model** — Source-system-neutral semantic/operating contract owned by CEPIO. The canonical model should remain thin relative to reusable Industry IQ.

**CEPIO** — Common Energy & Process Industry Ontology; productized as a portable Industrial Operating Intelligence layer for Energy & Process Industries.

**Competency question** — Frozen industrial question linked to persona/decision needs and declared CEPIO capability requirements.

**Compiled Industry IQ** — Deterministic runtime projection that compiles reusable Capability Registry content into bounded immutable activation bundles. It is acceleration, not semantic authority.

**Compounding Industrial Intelligence** — Governed process by which qualified reusable capability evidence can be proposed for reuse/promotion while preserving cross-domain regression, customer boundaries and human architectural review.

**ContextAssembler** — Runtime component that selects a bounded per-task working context from candidate memories/evidence under explicit budgets and fail-closed required-context rules.

**Customer Profile / Profile** — Customer/site/deployment specialization of CEPIO semantics, bindings, knowledge or operating logic. Profile knowledge must not silently become global Industry IQ.

**Decision** — First-class record connecting evidence/reasoning to a selected conclusion/course of action. Decision capability does not itself grant Action execution authority.

**DecisionDefinition** — Stable named runtime capability contract defining allowed Decision subject types and related governed Action vocabulary.

**Enterprise Performance Graph** — CEPIO performance-intelligence model connecting objectives, key results, KPIs, guardrails, accountability, contribution relationships and response pathways.

**Evidence** — Governed information supporting or contradicting a claim, assessment, Decision or actionability judgment. Evidence can reference authoritative source data without duplicating the underlying payload.

**Evidence bundle** — Scoped set of CEPIO evidence records available for a specific reasoning/agent execution. Citation membership alone does not prove entailment.

**Event** — Immutable occurrence that may contribute to deterministic state changes.

**FOUNDATION** — CEPIO v0.4 product capability label: coherent developer/runtime foundation.

**CONNECT** — CEPIO v0.5 product capability label: operation over authoritative industrial truth and one governed verified/reconciled external loop.

**EXECUTE** — CEPIO v0.6 product capability label: governed verified industrial outcomes through reference applications.

**EXTEND** — CEPIO v0.7 product capability label: independent teams can safely add Industry IQ/profile/binding capability.

**OPERATE** — CEPIO v0.8 product capability label: enterprise runtime dependability and operations guarantees.

**GENERALIZE** — CEPIO v0.9 product capability label: real portability/reuse across materially different enterprises/platform contexts.

**GUARANTEE** — CEPIO v1.0 product capability label: stable production contracts and explicit guarantees.

**Function** — Typed calculation or reasoning operation that does not itself imply an external side effect.

**Generalization** — Discipline testing whether CEPIO capability remains reusable across operating models/domains/customers/platform bindings without contaminating canonical semantics with customer-specific vocabulary.

**Governed Action** — Action whose identity, target, policy/authority, preconditions, idempotency, audit and external verification/reconciliation are enforced by CEPIO contracts.

**Industry IQ** — CEPIO's reusable industrial operating competence: semantics/compositions, personas, competencies, evidence requirements, Skills, failure/risk/decision patterns, constraints, performance/KPI/economic logic, workflows and evaluation/assurance contracts.

**Industry IQ Pack** — Versioned deployable package of reusable industrial intelligence under explicit dependency/trust/provenance contracts.

**Industry IQ Pack Registry** — Registry resolving deployed Industry IQ packs, versions, compatibility and capability collisions. Distinct from Capability Registry and Skill Registry.

**Industry Performance Reference Pack** — Organization-neutral reusable Performance Contract for an industrial archetype. Customers explicitly specialize formulas, targets, thresholds, scope, accountability and source bindings.

**InformationGain** — Assessment representing expected value/reduction of uncertainty from additional information.

**IRI** — Internationalized Resource Identifier; canonical semantic identity for ontology classes/properties/relationships.

**Kinetic plane** — Skills, Functions, Decisions, Actions, workflows and operational capability through which CEPIO moves from understanding to governed outcomes.

**Management Contract** — Separate CEPIO control-plane contract for sanitized runtime/capability observation. Current hosted remote projection is authenticated and read-only.

**MCP** — Model Context Protocol; one protocol projection through which authenticated agents can access governed CEPIO tools.

**Memory** — Durable CEPIO knowledge/evidence record distinct from per-task working context. Current logical kinds include Semantic, Procedural, Episodic and Profile memory.

**Model output** — Generated inference/prose/tool-selection result. Model output is not automatically evidence, authority or enterprise fact.

**Object** — Durable industrial or enterprise entity represented by CEPIO.

**Ontology module** — Versioned RDF/OWL package containing a coherent portion of CEPIO semantics.

**Operating abstraction / Industrial Operating Intelligence** — Stable governed layer through which humans/apps/agents understand and act on enterprise reality without coupling industrial logic directly to source-system or platform representations.

**Operational assurance** — Executable evidence that scoped runtime behavior satisfies temporal, provenance, authority, policy, Action/audit, Skill/agent or verified-outcome gates.

**Outcome / WorkflowOutcome** — Immutable governed record describing the terminal result of workflow/action execution and verification rather than inferring success from dispatch alone.

**Performance Contract** — Versioned CEPIO grammar for objectives, KRs, KPIs, guardrails, accountability, contributions and governed response bindings.

**Persona** — Industrial role definition with responsibilities/decisions/information needs. Persona context is distinct from authenticated policy authority.

**Profile memory** — Tenant/customer-owned durable memory that must not enter global/shared Industry IQ or shared compiled caches without explicit governed generalization.

**Projector** — Deterministic logic deriving operational state from immutable events.

**Property provenance** — Evidence showing where a property/state value originated and how/when it was observed, transformed and recorded.

**Recommendation** — Reasoned proposal favoring a course of action. Recommendation is not a Decision/authorization/execution grant.

**Reconciliation** — Process resolving CEPIO's record of an external Action with authoritative source-system truth, especially when write outcome is uncertain.

**Relationship** — Typed semantic connection between CEPIO objects, potentially with temporal history.

**Retrieval & Indexing** — Governed capability-routing and bounded candidate-selection layer between durable Industry IQ memory/evidence and ContextAssembler. Indexes are rebuildable projections rather than truth.

**Semantic capability readiness** — Deterministic satisfaction of declared ontology/capability prerequisites or approved alternative compositions; separate from runtime answerability or production readiness.

**Skill** — Versioned bounded industrial procedure declaring dependencies/evidence/Functions/Decision/Action needs and executed only after runtime/actor-aware readiness checks.

**Skill Registry** — Registry responsible for Skill admission/binding/readiness in a concrete runtime. Distinct from Industry IQ Pack Registry and Capability Registry.

**SourceReference** — CEPIO reference to authoritative source-system evidence/identity.

**State** — Current or historical operational condition of an object based on authoritative facts and/or deterministic event projection.

**Transaction time** — Time at which CEPIO recorded or learned a fact.

**UncertaintyAssessment** — First-class assessment representing incomplete knowledge/variability relevant to an industrial subject or Decision.

**Valid time** — Time at which a fact is true in the industrial domain.

**Verification** — Explicit check that an external Action produced the expected authoritative source state. Source acknowledgement is not necessarily verification.

**Working context** — Ephemeral bounded set of selected capability/evidence/memory material presented to a model/agent for one task; distinct from durable Industry IQ memory.

**North-Star invariants** — `Knowledge != Authority`, `Recommendation != Execution`, `Model Output != Evidence`, `Index != Source of Truth`, and `Cache != Authority`.
