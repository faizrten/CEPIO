# North Star documentation adoption register — 26 October 2026

**Purpose:** Trace the documentation alignment to the [canonical strategic doctrine](industry-iq-product-north-star.md). This is a documentation-only change, not a release qualification, change to operational controls or scorecard reweighting.

- **Canonical public doctrine:** mission, indispensable professional companion, thin-core/thick-IQ technology, Industrial Competence Network, sovereignty and compounding.
- **Primary navigation:** root README; docs index; strategy index; Wiki Home and Overview.

- **Trust and operations:** security/governance Wiki, glossary and Reliability Agent Lighthouse.
- **Private strategy:** corresponding CEPIO-Labs master, moat playbook, sovereignty doctrine and protected documentation index remain under protected review.

## Unchanged by design

- Historical OEDA 2024 reports, completed milestone artifacts, prior ADR decisions and old evaluation baselines remain immutable historical references.
- No runtime/API/ontology, permissions, Azure infrastructure, paid inference, PostgreSQL schema or action execution is introduced.
- No changes to the canonical 100-point scorecard numerical weights or earned points.
- No promotion or cross-tenant use of customer data, prompts, traces, synthetic derivatives or Outcomes is authorized.
- No claim that Companions, proactive attention, real agent actions, Cognite parity or expert marketplace offerings are live or qualified unless supported by their own evidence.

## Documentation governance

1. Product strategy claims refer to the canonical [Product North Star](industry-iq-product-north-star.md).
2. Release claims must refer to [current-state](../current-state.md), [roadmap](../roadmap.md), [RELEASE](../../RELEASE.md) and [scorecard](../v1-completion-scorecard.md).
3. Implemented behavior refers to accepted ADRs, contracts and tests.
4. Proprietary or customer-specific contents must not be copied into public documents; refer to appropriately classified records.
5. Future content should distinguish **aspiration**, **approved direction**, **implemented foundation**, **synthetic qualification** and **live field evidence**.
