## Six pillars

| Pillar | Authoritative statement | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mission — why** | **Democratize industrial expertise and elevate human professional excellence.** | Enable better professional judgment and capability; preserve accountable human decisions. |
| **Product North Star — what** | **The indispensable expert companion for every industrial professional.** | Useful in the flow of work: understand, prioritize, investigate, recommend, learn, and support governed outcomes. |
| **Technology — how** | **Thin infrastructure. Thick Industry IQ.** | Portable industrial operating intelligence, initially for Energy & Process Industries; reuse commodity infrastructure rather than reproducing it. |
| **Moat — what compounds** | **Industrial Competence Network.** | Versioned and independently qualified Skills, expert methods, decision/risk patterns, competence claims and assurance evidence. |
| **Sovereignty** | **Collective industrial progress. Sovereign enterprise intelligence.** | Compound lawfully sourced and qualified reusable competence without taking ownership of customer data, methods, decisions or derived intelligence. |
| **Compounding** | **Own the competence. Prove the competence. Distribute the competence. Compound the competence.** | Every promotion requires rights/provenance, independent evidence, regression and accountable approval, not merely more content. |

Our aspiration is that CEPIO becomes the trusted expert companion to industrial professionals across industries. **The present beachhead is Energy & Process Industries**, with an initial emphasis on reliability/maintenance, drilling and process operations. Cross-industry competence is a hypothesis to demonstrate, not a current claim.

## Product promise to a professional

CEPIO should help a professional:

1. **Understand my work:** role, responsibilities, assets, process, objectives, authorized enterprise context and relevant constraints.
2. **See what matters:** surface actionable, permissioned, evidence-backed attention rather than undifferentiated alerts.
3. **Investigate rigorously:** identify alternatives, evidence gaps, uncertainty, causal hypotheses and appropriate technical methods; abstain when unqualified.
4. **Complete governed work:** create traceable recommendations and Decisions; verify required authority before an Action; reconcile resulting Outcomes with authoritative systems.
5. **Learn and improve:** explain and challenge reasoning, strengthen professional judgment and preserve only appropriately scoped lessons.
6. **Trust and verify:** show provenance, freshness, authorization, qualification ceilings and explicit limitations.
7. **Work where I already am:** headless-first services through Teams, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Foundry-hosted agents, approved third-party hosts and an optional deeper Industry IQ Studio.

**No presumed autonomy:** CEPIO can enable proactive, bounded host agents but is not itself a substitute for human technical authority. Neither generated conclusions nor synthetic test fixtures are automatically field evidence.

```text
Industrial professional / authorized AI agent
       |

  industrial competence · Skills · evidence requirements

  KPI/economic reasoning · evaluations · Capability Passports
       |
CEPIO OPERATING CONTRACT — THIN / STABLE
  identity · semantics · state/time · evidence/provenance
  policy/authority · Decisions · Actions · verified Outcomes
       |
REPLACEABLE PLATFORM BINDINGS
  Fabric/Fabric IQ · Cognite · OSDU · authorized enterprise systems
       |

```

**Microsoft-native reference, not Microsoft-locked.** Microsoft Foundry/Copilot own generic agent hosting, model/tool loops, triggers, conversational interfaces and Teams approval UX; Fabric and Entra provide platform context and identity. CEPIO owns industrial operating meaning, admissible competence, evidence, Decisions, authority ceilings, Action contracts and Outcome semantics. **Microsoft owns agent execution. CEPIO owns industrial agency.** A non-Microsoft host must satisfy the same CEPIO operating contract, not require a fork.

**Engineering filter:** **Reuse → Bind → Extend → Own.** Reject generic CEPIO model hosting, agent frameworks, graph databases, search engines, vector stores, orchestration platforms or duplicated enterprise graphs without a clearly demonstrated industrial operating contract gap.

## Industrial Competence Network — our differentiated asset

A growing network of:
- **Industrial capability & Skill packages** that define professional tasks, required evidence, methods, outputs and authority limits;
- **Industrial casebooks, simulations and failure/risk patterns**, only when lawfully sourced, reviewed and qualified;
- **Independent assurance** with held-out/negative cases, matched-model baselines, abstention/safety checks, platform and model portability;
- **Professional workflow trust**, evidenced through actual use and useful decisions—not a claim inferred from installs or scenario counts;
- **Expert and OEM participation**, subject to contributor IP, publication rights, independent qualification and commercial agreements.

The **Capability Registry** records qualified claims. The **Industry IQ Pack Registry** records deployable packages. The **Skill Registry** controls executable procedure admission. None alone proves production readiness. A proposed **Capability Passport** should explain intended job, evidence, method, qualification scope, authority ceiling and limitations before users rely on a capability.

## Sovereignty and two compounding loops

**Global CEPIO competence loop:** independently owned/permitted source materials → protected synthetic scenarios and expert review → sealed independent evaluation → cross-domain regression → human-reviewed, versioned qualified release.

**Tenant-private outcome loop:** customer-authorized context → evidence-led investigation → Decisions and permissible Actions → source verification → customer-scoped Outcomes and lessons.

**No default cross-boundary learning.** Customer operational data, procedures, thresholds, telemetry, prompts, agent traces, embeddings, model adaptations, generated derivatives and Outcomes remain customer-controlled. Customer success does **not** grant CEPIO training, republication, evaluation or cross-tenant rights. Explicit optional contributions require independently assessed source rights, scope, provenance, confidentiality and human authorization. Fictionalized customer derivatives are not automatically unrestricted. Unknown permission fails closed.

See [ADR-0044](../adr/ADR-0044-sovereign-compounding-industrial-intelligence.md), [IP & Publication Policy](../governance/ip-publication-policy.md) and the enterprise-knowledge sovereignty ADRs. Proprietary methods, sealed evals and domain playbooks belong in **CEPIO-Labs**; customer material belongs in the customer's governed environment.

## What success means

Our ultimate measure is: **how much consequential industrial work can a professional accomplish more accurately, efficiently, safely and confidently because CEPIO is present?**

Measure qualification and attribution, not inventory alone:
- Matched-data/model **Industry IQ uplift** versus the same model and evidence/tools without CEPIO competence;
- evidenced, independent **reusable competency breadth** and cross-domain/platform/model generalization;
- abstention quality, material-hallucination rate, denied/prohibited Actions, evidence provenance and source verification;
- workflow usefulness and **professional trust/adoption** over time;
- verified operational Outcomes and attributable time, reliability, risk or economic benefit where legitimate baseline and customer permission exist;
- **sovereignty assurance**, including zero unauthorized cross-tenant movement of knowledge or derivatives.

Maintain these as strategic indicators without inventing scores. The numerical v1 maturity authority is [config/v1-scorecard.yaml](../../config/v1-scorecard.yaml), whose evidence and weights require their own governance.

## Road to proof

1. **Reliability lighthouse:** Microsoft-hosted agent → CEPIO MCP → admitted Industry IQ Skill → governed evidence → persisted Decision. Offline qualification is not a live Foundry PASS.
2. **Bounded enterprise agency:** mandate/capability and actor authority → event/attention → Teams approval where required → governed source Action → read-back/verification → Outcome.
3. **Industrial assurance:** prove Industry IQ adds value on independent cases relative to matched model/evidence baselines; negative tests and failed qualifications remain visible.
4. **Portability:** same qualified capability across Fabric and Cognite bindings without Core change or duplicating source graphs.
5. **Enterprise and professional proof:** tenant specialization, consent/rights and security boundaries, repeat usefulness, cross-enterprise reuse and observable outcomes.

Gate owners and release definitions remain in the [execution roadmap](../roadmap.md); implementation and independently qualified claims must not be conflated with a vision statement.
